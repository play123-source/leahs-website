Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

You can self-refer for all services. Some services may require a GP referral or treatment plan for Medicare rebates.

How do I get a Medicare mental health treatment plan (MHTP)?

To access Medicare rebates, you’ll need to book a longer appointment with your GP to discuss a MHTP. Your GP will discuss your reasons for seeking support and may ask you to complete a few short questionnaires to assess your eligibility. While your GP may recommend a particular psychologist, you’re free to choose who you see. Your referral can be used with any eligible psychologist, so you can choose the practitioner you feel is the right fit for you.

What happens at the first appointment?

The first session is an opportunity to understand what you’re experiencing and why, address any questions, discuss confidentiality, and develop a treatment plan together. It’s also a chance to ensure you feel understood and comfortable working with me to move forward.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Telehealth appointments are available across Australia and are provided through secure online platforms.

What age groups do you support?

Services are available for clients aged 15+.

How do I know you're the right psychologist for me?

Finding the right psychologist is an important part of the therapy process, and I want you to feel confident that my approach is right for you. Our free 10-minute initial phone call is an opportunity to briefly discuss my style and approach, ask any questions you may have, and get a sense of whether we might work well together.

A strong therapeutic relationship is an important part of effective therapy. You should feel understood, respected, and comfortable with both your psychologist and their approach. I welcome feedback about how I can best support you, and I recognise that my approach may not be the right fit for everyone. If that’s the case, that’s absolutely okay. I encourage you to be open about what you need, so you can find the support that feels right for you.

Is your practice inclusive of LGBTQIA+ clients?

We welcome people of all sexual orientations, gender identities, and backgrounds. Leah aims to provide a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment where you can feel comfortable being yourself, without fear of judgement or discrimination.

How do I book?

Please see our booking instructions here or reach out to us via our contact us page if you need assistance.

Can I receive a Medicare rebate for couples counselling?

Unfortunately, Medicare does not provide rebates for couples therapy. Some private health providers may provide rebates for couples therapy. Please check with your provider directly.

How long is the waitlist?

Wait times vary through the year. We actively triage and encourage urgent clients to contact us directly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least two business days' notice, otherwise, late cancellation fees will apply. More information is available on our Fees page.
