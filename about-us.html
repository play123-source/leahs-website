About Us

Leah is a Psychologist and Clinical Psychology Registrar who brings a warm, genuine, and practical approach to psychological care.

Before moving into psychology, Leah had a longstanding corporate career, where she led global teams and worked closely with people and organisations to navigate complex challenges, achieve their goals and reach their potential. Leah found that what she valued most was always the people, understanding their experiences, recognising their strengths, and helping them move forward.

This ultimately led Leah to pursue a career in psychology, combining her extensive experience working with people with her passion for understanding human behaviour, wellbeing, and meaningful change.

As a psychologist, Leah provides a non-judgemental, respectful and collaborative space where you can feel comfortable being yourself. She believes therapy should be tailored to the individual, rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach. Leah will work with you to understand what is happening, identify the patterns that may be keeping you stuck, and develop practical, evidence-based strategies to help you move towards the life you want.

Her approach draws on Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Schema Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, and strengths-based approaches.

Qualifications: Master of Clinical Psychology, Master of Psychological Practice, Bachelor of Social Science (Psychology), and Master of Business Administration (Computing).
