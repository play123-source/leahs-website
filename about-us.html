About Us

Leah is a registered psychologist and clinical psychology registrar who brings a warm, genuine, and practical approach to psychological care.

Before pursuing a career in psychology, Leah spent many years working in the corporate sector, holding leadership and senior roles within large multinational organisations, including Microsoft, Orion Health, and Daon. During this time, she led global teams and worked with people across diverse industries and cultures, navigating complex challenges, organisational change, and professional demands.

Through these experiences, Leah developed a strong appreciation for the human side of work and life: understanding people's experiences, recognising their strengths, and supporting them through challenges and change. This ultimately inspired her transition into psychology, bringing together her extensive professional experience, leadership background, and longstanding interest in human behaviour, wellbeing, and personal growth.

Leah provides a respectful, non-judgemental, and collaborative space where you can feel comfortable being yourself. She believes therapy should be tailored to the individual, rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach. Together, you will explore what is happening in your life, identify patterns that may be keeping you stuck, and develop practical, evidence-based strategies to help you move towards the life you want to lead.

Her approach draws on Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Schema Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, Gottman Method Couples Therapy, and strengths-based approaches. She works collaboratively with her clients to find an approach that reflects their individual needs, goals, and circumstances.

Outside of work, Leah enjoys spending time with her family, travelling, mountain biking, hiking, and getting lost in a good book.

Qualifications: Master of Clinical Psychology, Master of Psychological Practice, Master of Business Administration (Computing), and Bachelor of Social Science (Psychology).
