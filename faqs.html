Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

You can self-refer for many services. Some funding pathways may require a GP referral or treatment plan.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Telehealth appointments are available across Australia and are provided through secure online platforms.

What age groups do you support?

Services are available for adults, adolescents, children, and families, depending on presenting needs and service fit.

How long is the waitlist?

Wait times vary through the year. We actively triage and encourage urgent clients to contact us directly.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours notice when possible. Late cancellation fees may apply.

How do I book?

You can book online through our booking page or contact us for help with selecting the right appointment type.

Important: Crisis Support

We do not provide emergency or crisis response services. If you need urgent support, call 000 or contact one of the services below.

  • Suicide Call Back Service: 1300 659 467
  • Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636
  • Kids Helpline: 1800 55 1800
