About Us

Within Psychology and Assessment is led by Leah, a Provisional Psychologist and Master of Clinical Psychology candidate with a passion for strengths-based, inclusive, and evidence-based practice.

She currently practices at Unify Health and previously completed a fifth-year clinical placement at Black Feather Psychology, delivering assessments and interventions across the lifespan.

Before transitioning fully into psychology, Leah spent over two decades in senior leadership roles across health and technology, building deep capability in complex systems, stakeholder engagement, and sustainable outcomes. This background supports her practical, grounded therapeutic approach.

Leah completed a Master of Psychological Practice and a Bachelor of Social Science (Psychology), and is now completing a Master of Clinical Psychology at Charles Sturt University.
