Assessments

Our practice is built on a foundation of neuro-affirming care and trauma-informed practice, offering assessments that centre your voice and well-being. Whether you are seeking clarity, education/workplace accommodations, or self-understanding, please reach out to us with your specific needs. We are able to offer assessment services to children aged 8+, adolescents, and adults.

ADHD/Autism Assessments

We offer comprehensive ADHD and Autism assessments. Our assessments are developmentally informed, supportive, and have actionable recommendations.

Cognitive/Intelligence Assessments

We offer comprehensive cognitive and intelligence testing to help uncover an individual’s unique learning profile. Rather than just focusing on scores, our assessments map out clear cognitive strengths and areas where extra support may be needed, providing practical recommendations for school, work, or daily life.

Personality Assessments

We offer personality assessments designed to help you gain a deeper understanding of your behavioural patterns, emotional style, and interpersonal dynamics. This process offers validating insights into your unique traits, helping you build self-awareness and practical tools for daily life.
