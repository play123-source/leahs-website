Within Psychology and Assessment is a compassionate, evidence-based psychological practice in Springfield, Queensland, providing in-person support and telehealth services across Australia.

We support young people aged 15+ and adults, as well as couples, across a range of concerns including anxiety and mood, trauma, neurodivergence, relationships, substance use, burnout and life transitions. We also provide psychological services through WorkCover and NDIS.

We believe meaningful psychological care begins with understanding the whole person. Our approach is collaborative, evidence-based and tailored to each individual, helping clients develop greater insight, build practical skills and move towards the life they want to live.

Important: Crisis Support

Within Psychology and Assessment does not provide crisis support. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please call 000 or access one of the services below:

  • Lifeline (24/7): 13 11 14 or lifeline.org.au
  • Suicide Call Back Service (24/7): 1300 659 467 or suicidecallbackservice.org.au
  • Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636 or beyondblue.org.au
  • Kids Helpline (24/7): 1800 55 1800 or kidshelpline.com.au
