Fees

Transparent fee information helps you plan your care with confidence. Our fees are set with consideration of the Australian Psychological Society (APS) National Schedule of Suggested Fees. For 2026–27, the APS suggested fee for a standard consultation is $330. Our fees are set below the APS suggested fee to help make psychological support more accessible. For new clients, you must pay your first session in full, 48 hours before your appointment.

Assessments

Mental Health Treatment Plan Rebate

You may be eligible for a Medicare mental health treatment plan (MHTP). Please see your GP for a referral, which may enable you to receive a Medicare rebate of $101.55 per session. More information here: https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/mental-health-treatment-plan.

Non-Directive Pregnancy Support

With a valid referral, you will be entitled to receive a Medicare rebate of $74.40 for three sessions in a 12-month period.

Private Health Rebates

If you have private health, please ask your provider whether you are eligible for a rebate. Please note, you cannot claim a Medicare and private health fund rebate simultaneously.

Cancellation Policy

We understand that unexpected circumstances can arise and sometimes appointments need to be cancelled or rescheduled. Please provide as much notice as possible, as this allows us to offer the appointment to someone else who may be waiting for support. Our cancellation fees are:

  • 48 hours or more notice: No fee
  • 24–48 hours notice: 50% of the consultation fee
  • Less than 24 hours notice: 100% of the consultation fee

Cancellation fees are not claimable through Medicare and are not covered by WorkCover Queensland.

We understand that circumstances can vary, and we will consider exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis.
